--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  STEP 1 -- PREPROCESSING
    a) Bandpass filter        : 0.1 Hz to 30 Hz (remove DC drift + high-freq noise)
    b) Baseline correction    : Subtract mean of -200ms to 0ms window per epoch
    c) Artifact removal       : Run ICA -- remove eye-blink components (IC on FP1/AF3)
    d) Epoch rejection        : Remove remaining epochs with amplitude > 100 uV

  STEP 2 -- FEATURE EXTRACTION (choose one)
    Option A (Simple):   Raw amplitude at Pz + Cz in 300-500ms window
    Option B (Better):   xDAWN spatial filter --> reduces noise, amplifies P300

  STEP 3 -- CLASSIFICATION
    - Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) : fast, interpretable, works well for P300

    - SWLDA (Stepwise LDA)               : classic BCI2000 P300 speller algorithm
